⭐ Enterprise Requirements

The Advertisement Manager must remain provider-agnostic, allowing support for current and future ad networks through plugins.

The Theme Builder and Widget System should follow the same modular architecture introduced in Phase 2 – Part A.

All advertisement rendering should prioritize performance, security, and policy compliance.

The design must support future monetization features without requiring changes to the CMS core.
