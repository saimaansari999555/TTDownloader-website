---

FINAL QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

The design specification should:

Be implementation-ready.

Avoid ambiguity.

Be reusable.

Scale to future features.

Support plugins, themes, widgets, multilingual interfaces, and responsive layouts without redesign.



---

⭐ Enterprise Requirements

Use an Atomic Design methodology (Atoms, Molecules, Organisms, Templates, Pages).

Ensure the design system is compatible with modern frontend frameworks such as React/Next.js.

Every component must support light mode, dark mode, localization (LTR/RTL), and accessibility from the beginning.

The UI specification should remain extensible so future modules can be added without breaking visual consistency.
