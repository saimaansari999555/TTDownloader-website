ENTERPRISE REQUIREMENTS (Highest Priority)

This application must be developed as a production-grade SaaS-ready platform, not as a demo project.

The codebase should be modular, well-documented, secure by default, horizontally scalable, and cloud-ready. All critical systems (authentication, backups, logging, monitoring, and deployment) should be designed to support long-term maintenance and future expansion with minimal refactoring.

The final project should be capable of evolving into a multi-service platform while preserving clean architecture and high performance.
